// Services

What We Do

In short: we try to break in before someone else does, then show you exactly how to lock the door.

Penetration Testing

Spot the holes before they get picked. Detailed testing across your infrastructure and applications, with clear remediation guidance.

In plain terms: like hiring someone to try every door and window in your house, safely and with permission, so you find the weak ones before a burglar does.

Red Teaming

Full adversary simulation. We test your defenses end-to-end under realistic threat scenarios, measuring resilience not just compliance.

In plain terms: a full dress rehearsal for a real break-in, testing your locks, your alarms, and how your team reacts under pressure.

Phishing Campaigns

Controlled simulations that test and train employees. Build real awareness, not just checkbox compliance.

In plain terms: we send safe, fake scam emails to your team to see who clicks, then teach everyone how to spot the real ones.

Consulting & Training

Practical, direct, and quick. No-fluff advice on hardening your security posture, with training that actually sticks.

In plain terms: straight talk on how to protect yourself or your business, no jargon, just steps you can actually follow.

Secure Code Reviews

Clear feedback, nothing cryptic. We review your codebase for security weaknesses and deliver actionable fixes.

In plain terms: if software is being built for you, we check it for hidden weak spots before it goes live, like a home inspector checking behind the walls.

// Why It Matters

Is This For Me?

Short answer: yes, if you have anything worth protecting. Here's why, in plain terms.

For Individuals

If you're a public figure, executive, or simply someone with money, a reputation, or private information, you're a more attractive target.

In plain terms: criminals go after people who look like a big payoff. We find out what a hacker could see or steal about you, and help you close those gaps, before it happens.

For Businesses

One weak password, one unpatched system, or one convincing scam email can lead to stolen customer data, drained accounts, or weeks of downtime.

In plain terms: it's cheaper to pay us to find the gaps now than to pay for a break-in later, in lost money, lost trust, and lost time.
